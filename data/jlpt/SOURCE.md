# JLPT vocabulary lists

These lists are by Jonathan Waller, originally published at tanos.co.uk, and are
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were taken from the parsed copies in
https://github.com/Bluskyo/JLPT_Vocabulary and changed as follows:

- converted to `word,reading,level` columns, one file per level
- rows listing several spellings or readings split into separate rows
- the corrupted N3 reading for 賛成 corrected to さんせい
- a few malformed rows removed
